# Figure checklist

An example skill. Orbit loads a skill on demand when your request matches it, so
the procedure sits out of the way until it is needed. Delete this or replace it
with your own; `save_skill` also lets Orbit write one mid-conversation.

## When to use
Before exporting any figure that leaves your machine — a paper, a talk, a report.

## Steps
1. Axes carry units and a scale. No bare numbers.
2. Every colour that means something appears in a legend or a direct label.
3. n is stated for anything aggregated, in the caption, not only in the methods.
4. Error bars say what they are: SD, SEM, or CI, and over what.
5. Export at 300 dpi or as vector. Check the smallest text is still legible at
   the size it will actually be printed.
6. Open the exported file and look at it. Rendering differs from the preview.

## How to tell it worked
Someone who has not seen the data can say what the figure claims, and what the
error bars mean, without asking you.
